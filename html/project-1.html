Overview

A brand as organic as the work itself.

Studio Bloom came to me with a clear vision but no visual language to match it. They needed a brand identity that felt refined yet alive — something that conveyed craftsmanship and nature without leaning into cliché florals or overly feminine tropes.

The result is a restrained system built on a warm off-white palette, a custom logotype with organic letterform details, and a set of hand-drawn botanical marks used as flexible supporting elements across collateral.

Process

How we got there.

01

Discovery & Brand Audit

Three sessions with the founder to understand her vision, clientele, and competitive landscape. We reviewed reference imagery together to align on aesthetic direction before any design work began.

02

Concept Development

I explored two distinct directions — one rooted in minimal Swiss typography, the other in softer editorial aesthetics. The client chose a hybrid approach, retaining the clean structure with subtle organic warmth.

03

Logo & Type System

The wordmark was hand-drawn then digitised, with subtle ink-trap details in the letterforms. Paired with a secondary serif for body copy and a light sans-serif for captions and UI contexts.

04

Collateral & Guidelines

Applied the system to business cards, packaging tissue, care cards, and a photographer brief. Delivered a 32-page brand guidelines document covering all use cases.

Outcomes

The impact.

Within three months of launch, Studio Bloom reported a significant uplift in inquiry quality — attracting clients aligned with their premium positioning. The brand has since been featured in two regional lifestyle publications.
