Hefei Firefighting Awareness Campaign

A public awareness campaign for Hefei's fire safety initiative — bold, urgent visuals designed to educate and engage a broad civic audience across outdoor, print, and digital channels.

Role

Visual Designer

Timeline

6 Weeks

Deliverables

Posters, Digital, OOH

Year

2025

Overview

Design that saves lives.

The Hefei Municipal Fire Department commissioned a public awareness campaign to improve fire safety knowledge among residents — particularly in high-density residential areas where response times are critical and awareness gaps are most dangerous.

The brief called for visuals that could cut through the noise of everyday city life: striking enough to stop someone on the street, clear enough to communicate quickly, and warm enough to feel approachable rather than alarmist.

Process

Designing urgency without panic.

01

Audience Research

Reviewed existing public safety communication in China and internationally. Identified that most fire safety campaigns relied on fear-based imagery — an approach that reduces engagement and retention. Chose to pursue a warmer, action-oriented tone.

02

Visual Direction

Developed a system using bold flat illustration with a limited palette of warm red, deep charcoal, and off-white. Each poster centred on a single clear action — extinguish, evacuate, call — avoiding information overload.

03

Poster & Asset Design

Designed a series of six hero posters and a set of supporting digital assets. Typography was set large and bold in both Simplified Chinese and a companion Latin face, ensuring legibility at distance for outdoor placements.

04

Production & Handoff

Prepared print-ready files for large-format outdoor, A2 community notice boards, and digital social formats. Provided the client with an editable template set for future seasonal updates.

Final Product

The posters, installed.

Six hero posters installed across Hefei's residential districts — community centres, elevator lobbies, and bus shelters — each designed to communicate a single, clear fire safety action at a glance.

Outcomes

Visible across the city.

The campaign was installed across Hefei's residential districts in Q3 2025. The department reported positive community feedback and plans to extend the campaign into additional districts.
