Campaign Design

A UX case study exploring onboarding and risk orientation flows for Hive — simplifying complex financial risk information into a clear, human-centred experience that guides users with confidence.

Role

UX/UI Designer

Timeline

5 Weeks

Deliverables

Research, Wireframes, Prototype

Year

2025

Overview

Making risk feel less risky.

Hive is a fintech platform helping users navigate investment and financial risk. Their existing onboarding flow was technically functional but emotionally flat — users dropped off during the risk orientation questionnaire at a high rate, overwhelmed by jargon and the length of the process.

This case study explores how a redesigned orientation flow — grounded in plain language, progressive disclosure, and reassuring visual design — could reduce drop-off and build genuine user confidence before they make any financial decisions.

Process

Understanding before designing.

01

Problem Definition & User Research

Analysed the existing flow using heuristic evaluation and reviewed drop-off analytics. Conducted 6 user interviews to understand where confusion and anxiety peaked. Found three critical friction points: question phrasing, progress uncertainty, and lack of result explanation.

02

Information Architecture

Restructured the questionnaire from a 12-question single-scroll format into a 5-step conversational flow. Rewrote question copy in plain language, removing financial jargon and replacing abstract risk scores with relatable scenario framing.

03

Wireframing & Iteration

Built low-fidelity wireframes and tested with 5 participants. Two rounds of iteration focused on the progress indicator design and the result reveal screen — the moment users learn their risk profile — which needed to feel affirming rather than judgmental.

04

High-Fidelity Prototype

Produced a fully interactive Figma prototype covering the complete orientation flow, including edge cases and error states. Documented design decisions and accessibility considerations in a case study presentation delivered to the Hive team.

Final Product

The redesigned flow.

A five-step conversational orientation experience — from a welcoming intro screen through to a clear, affirming risk profile result. Every screen designed to reduce anxiety and build informed confidence.

Outcomes

Clarity builds confidence.

Usability testing of the redesigned flow showed a marked improvement in task completion and user sentiment. Participants reported feeling more informed and less anxious at the end of the orientation.
